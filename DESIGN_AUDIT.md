# Design audit: Dots → Airloom

Reference: [ChatGPT Dots](https://chatgpt.com/features/dots/) (reviewed November 23, 2026). This is a visual study of the page's layout and interaction patterns. Airloom uses its own name, copy, SVG mark, illustration, and product UI.

| Pattern in the reference | Why it works | Airloom adaptation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Nearly black page with white type | The hero feels focused; images and product moments carry the colour. | Use a flat charcoal canvas throughout the landing page. Let live strings and sound illustrations provide colour. |
| Single floating object above a centered headline | Gives the page one memorable focal point before the product media. | Use an original three-string SVG mark that suggests movement and sound. |
| Large, neutral headline and muted supporting line | Creates a clear hierarchy without decorative labels. | “Introducing Airloom” leads; one short sentence explains the instrument. |
| White primary pill and outlined secondary pill | One action is obvious while the other remains easy to find. | “Start playing” jumps to the instrument; “Explore the code” opens GitHub. |
| Large rounded media surface directly after the hero | The product experience becomes the next chapter of the page. | The working camera/mouse instrument takes the place of a passive video. |
| Alternating text and image sections | Explains capabilities through a steady, readable rhythm. | Three gesture steps alternate concise copy with original code-drawn string illustrations. |
| On mobile, actions stack and the media appears early | Keeps the first interaction within reach on a narrow screen. | The two pills stack; the instrument starts just below the introduction. |

The instrument uses a separate dark surface so webcam frames remain legible. Its controls keep higher contrast than the surrounding page, and mouse mode remains available when camera permission is denied or a webcam is absent.
